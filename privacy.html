Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026.

Droplet Island is a Windows desktop application. It has no servers and no user accounts of its own, and it collects no telemetry.

Google Calendar data

If you connect a Google account, the application reads your calendar list and events, and creates events you ask it to add. It also reads your account's email address to tell connected accounts apart.

  • Events are stored only on your computer, in the application's local database, so they can be shown offline and used for reminders.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on your computer, in Windows Credential Manager.
  • This data is exchanged only between your computer and Google. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else, is not sold, and is not used for advertising or for training models.

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Signing out of an account in the application's settings deletes its token and all its events from your computer and revokes the access at Google. You can also revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
